CARLOTTA TONINI BACK TO GRAPHIC DESIGN

Kartell X Cruella

CONCEPT

The project brief required the creation of a series of three posters, developed within a one-week time frame, that would convey the identity of an assigned Italian company through the use of three frames taken from a film of our choice.

Our project focused on Kartell, a company renowned for its colorful aesthetic that has remained consistent over time. We aimed to capture and communicate its personality—bold and true to itself. For this reason, we drew a parallel with the character of Cruella, from Craig Gillespie’s 2021 film Cruella.

In this scene, Cruella reveals her red dress in a dramatic moment, as flames consume the white overcoat that had concealed her and conformed her to the black-and-white theme of the party she is attending. To convey the explosive nature of this revelation, the image was treated with graphic elements such as halftones and print screens, evoking a comic book style that also nods to the POP aesthetic—a hallmark of Kartell’s identity.

“What says Kartell better
than behing bold?”
