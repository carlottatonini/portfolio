Kreuzweise 2026

CONCEPT

Kreuzweise 2026 is a youth media initiative in Saxony-Anhalt where participants aged 18 - 26 turn their views on political and social issues into short films, with selected projects professionally developed alongside local community TV stations.

For this edition, I handled the process of producing the official trailer, from the ideation and writing, all throughout the production and post-production phases.

The trailer is an audiovisual call-to-action that nods at young people to get up from their seats, even when the world and news feel overwhelming, to finally have a space dedicated to them, their opinions and their thoughts.

“What would it take to convince you to stand up and speak?”
